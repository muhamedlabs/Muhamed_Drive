## Security & Privacy

Muhamed Drive provides comprehensive protection for your data at all levels. We combine modern cryptographic standards, strict access policies, and transparency in information handling.

- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Two-factor authentication 2FA (TOTP/SMS)

**Core guarantees:**

- **End-to-end encryption (E2EE)** for files stored in personal vaults. Encryption keys are generated client-side and never transmitted to the server.

- **Zero-Knowledge policy** – Muhamed Drive administrators have no access to your file contents. Even if requested by law enforcement, we cannot provide access to encrypted data.

- **Regular audits and penetration testing** by independent firms. Security reports are available to enterprise clients under NDA.

- **Automatic suspicious activity blocking** (repeated failed login attempts, anomalous geolocation, new devices). Users receive email and in-app notifications.

- **Remote wipe** for lost or compromised devices.

- **Detailed activity log** (who, when, from which IP, and which file was opened/uploaded/deleted). Logs are retained for 180 days.

- **ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II** certification for data centers.

**Legal aspects:**

- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Ukrainian Personal Data Protection Law.
- Data is stored exclusively on servers within the European Union (Frankfurt and Amsterdam) with backup in Switzerland.
- You may sign a DPA or request deletion of all your data at any time.

> We recommend enabling two-factor authentication and using a password manager to generate unique account passwords.

## Future Technologies: What Makes Muhamed Drive Unique?

- **AI File Organizer** – automatic sorting and tagging of uploaded documents, photos, and videos. Search by image content (OCR) and voice query input.

- **Quantum-resistant encryption (hybrid post-quantum cryptography)** – protection against attacks from future quantum computers. We have implemented CRYSTALS-Kyber and CRYSTALS-Dilithium algorithms.

- **Green data centers** – 98% of the energy consumed by Muhamed Drive servers comes from renewable sources (solar and wind farms in Germany and the Netherlands). Carbon footprint is offset by tree planting (1 tree for every 10 TB of uploaded data).

- **Built-in antivirus & sandbox** – all files are scanned for malware before storage. Suspicious executable files are isolated in a sandbox and cannot harm other users.

- **Smart Sync** – partial file synchronization: you see all files in your file explorer, but they only take up space in the cloud. Only the files you open are downloaded. Save up to 90% of local disk space.

- **WebDAV + Rclone compatibility** – mount Muhamed Drive as a network drive or use it for automated server backups.
